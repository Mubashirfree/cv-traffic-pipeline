    Known limits

    • Vehicle counts come from an automatic detector, so they can be off in dense traffic or at night.
    • The Indian training photos contain no video, so tracking and clip search on videos was checked by eye, not scored.
    • Colour is easily lost when many vehicles are averaged together.
    • Explanations are written by an AI model from the detector's counts. They are checked automatically for numbers that do not match.
